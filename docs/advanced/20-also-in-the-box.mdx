:::info[🧳 In Wayfinder]

Wayfinder customers shouldn't see a sign-in screen every time they open the app. A **session checkpoint** in the sign-in flow lets a returning customer skip the password step until the session expires (by default after 2 hours idle or 8 hours in total).

:::
