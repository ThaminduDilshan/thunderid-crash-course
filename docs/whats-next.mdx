You've finished the course. Here's where to go from here.

- **Build something real.** Pick a [use case](https://thunderid.dev/docs/next/use-cases/overview) (B2C, B2B, AI agents or verifiable credentials) and take its sample further than the walkthroughs go.
- **Read a design.** Newer features have a spec, and some a threat model, in [`docs-internals/`](https://github.com/thunder-id/thunderid/tree/main/docs-internals). The design reasoning for many others is in the [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/thunder-id/thunderid/discussions). Read both for the area you'll work on.
- **Contribute.** The [community guide](https://thunderid.dev/community/overview) covers dev setup, debugging and the pull request workflow. Then browse [good first issues](https://github.com/thunder-id/thunderid/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22).
- **Say hello.** Join `#thunderid-dev` on the [LF Decentralized Trust Discord](https://discord.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/).

Check [My progress](/progress) for any module you skipped.
